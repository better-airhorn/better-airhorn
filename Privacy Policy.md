# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

Better Airhorn ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information about users of the Better Airhorn Discord Bot (the "Bot").

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Discord IDs
We collect and store Discord IDs, which include user IDs, server IDs, and message IDs. This information is necessary for the Bot to function correctly and provide a personalized experience.

### 1.2 Audio Files
We collect and store audio files uploaded by users for use with the Bot. These files are used to enhance the functionality of the Bot and provide a better user experience.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 Provide and Improve the Bot
We use the collected information to operate, maintain, and improve the Bot's functionality. This includes using Discord IDs to manage user interactions and audio files to deliver requested audio playback services.

### 2.2 Legal Compliance
We may use your information to comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.

## 3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

### 3.1 Service Providers
We do not share your information with third-party service providers. All data is stored and processed solely for the purpose of providing the Bot's services.

### 3.2 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or a government agency).

### 3.3 Protection of Rights
We may disclose your information to protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property, and/or that of our users, including to enforce our Terms of Service.

## 4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain the information we collect for as long as necessary to provide the Bot's services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. If you wish to request the deletion of your data, please contact us at [here](https://discord.gg/5bfhkJ3) or message chy4e on Discord.
When requesting deletion of data via commands, the data as well as audio files are deleted instantly and permanently.

## 6. Your Rights

### 6.1 Access and Update Information
You have the right to access and update your information stored by the Bot. If you need assistance, please contact us at at [here](https://discord.gg/5bfhkJ3) or message chy4e on Discord.

### 6.2 Delete Information
You have the right to request the deletion of your information. To do so, please contact us at at [here](https://discord.gg/5bfhkJ3) or message chy4e on Discord, and we will take reasonable steps to delete your information from our records.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.
