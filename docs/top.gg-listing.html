What you can do

🔊
/play

Search as you type, typos included. Leave it empty and it suggests sounds based on your likes.

🎛️
/panel

Pin a board of up to 20 buttons in any channel. One click plays the sound; /panel repost brings it back when chat buries it.

⬆️
/upload

mp3, wav, ogg, flac, m4a and more. Trim it in the form. Every sound is levelled, so nothing blasts your ears.

🚪
/entrance

Your sound plays when you join a voice channel.

💾
Save as sound

Right-click a voice message or audio file, then Apps. It's in your library a few seconds later.

⭐
/export

Cut a sound to Discord's limits and add it to your server's built-in soundboard.

Also: /share posts a sound as a file (in DMs too, once you add the app to your account), /random picks one for you, likes rank the best sounds, and each upload is public, server-only or private.

Get started

  1. Add the bot and join a voice channel.
  2. Type /play and start typing a name, or try /random.
  3. /upload your first sound, then /panel create to pin a board for your server.

Storage and votes

Everyone gets 250 MB (10 MB per sound), which is hours of audio. A vote here raises that to 750 MB (25 MB per sound) for 7 days. Playing is never limited by votes.
